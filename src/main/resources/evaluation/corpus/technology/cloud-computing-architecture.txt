overview: Cloud computing delivers on-demand computing services over the internet with elastic scaling and resource pooling.

## Service and Deployment Models
Cloud architectures provide Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), and Software as a Service (SaaS) deployed across public, private, and hybrid clouds.

## Distributed Consensus and Microservices
Microservice architectures decompose applications into independently deployable services coordinating via asynchronous messaging and distributed consensus protocols (Raft, Paxos).
