## Superposition and Entanglement
A quantum bit (qubit) can exist in a superposition of basis states |0> and |1>. Entanglement creates correlated quantum states across multiple qubits with non-classical properties.

## Quantum Algorithms
Shor algorithm factors integers in polynomial time on quantum computers, while Grover search algorithm finds entries in an unstructured database with quadratic O(sqrt(N)) speedup.
