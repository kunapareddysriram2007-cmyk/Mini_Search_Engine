title: NoSQL Databases and Distributed Document Stores
overview: NoSQL databases provide flexible schema designs, horizontal scaling, and specialized data models for web-scale workloads.

## NoSQL Data Models
NoSQL systems encompass document stores (MongoDB, Couchbase), key-value databases (Redis, DynamoDB), wide-column stores (Cassandra), and graph databases (Neo4j).

## The CAP Theorem and Consistency
The CAP theorem states that a distributed data store can simultaneously provide at most two of Consistency, Availability, and Partition tolerance. Many NoSQL systems adopt eventual consistency.
