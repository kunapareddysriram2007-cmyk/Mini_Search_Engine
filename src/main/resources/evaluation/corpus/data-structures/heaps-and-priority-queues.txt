title: Heaps and Priority Queue Implementations
overview: A heap is a specialized tree-based data structure that satisfies the heap property, powering priority queues.

## Binary Heap Properties
In a min-heap, every parent node is less than or equal to its children, placing the minimum element at the root. Binary heaps are compactly represented inside contiguous arrays.

## Operations and Applications
Insertion and extract-min operate in O(log n) time, while heap building takes O(n). Priority queues built on heaps power CPU scheduling, Dijkstra shortest path, and Huffman coding.
