title: Balanced Binary Search Trees: Red-Black and AVL
overview: Self-balancing binary search trees maintain O(log n) height guarantees through tree rotations during insert and delete.

## AVL Tree Strict Balance
AVL trees strictly maintain a balance factor of at most 1 between left and right subtrees, providing faster lookup times at the expense of more frequent tree rotations during updates.

## Red-Black Tree Properties
Red-Black trees use node coloring rules (root is black, no consecutive red nodes, equal black-height) to guarantee that the longest path is at most twice the length of the shortest path.
