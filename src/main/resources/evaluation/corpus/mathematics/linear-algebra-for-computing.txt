title: Linear Algebra for Computing and Data Science
overview: Linear algebra is the branch of mathematics concerning vector spaces, linear mappings, and matrix transformations.

## Vectors, Matrices and Dot Products
Vector dot products compute inner products and geometric angles between multi-dimensional vectors, providing the mathematical basis for TF-IDF cosine similarity calculations in search engines.

## Matrix Factorization and Eigenvalues
Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) and Principal Component Analysis (PCA) factorize high-dimensional matrices to extract latent semantic structures and compress feature spaces.
