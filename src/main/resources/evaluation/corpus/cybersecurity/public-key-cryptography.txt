title: Public Key Cryptography and Digital Certificates
overview: Public-key cryptography uses mathematically linked public and private key pairs for secure encryption and identity authentication.

## Asymmetric Algorithms: RSA and ECC
RSA relies on the computational difficulty of factoring large prime numbers, while Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC) provides equivalent cryptographic security with shorter keys.

## Digital Signatures and TLS Handshake
Digital signatures provide message integrity and non-repudiation. Transport Layer Security (TLS) uses asymmetric encryption during handshakes to securely negotiate symmetric session keys.
