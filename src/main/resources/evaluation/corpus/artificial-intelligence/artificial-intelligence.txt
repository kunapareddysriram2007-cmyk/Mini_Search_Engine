overview: Artificial intelligence (AI) is the science and engineering of making intelligent machines and software capable of reasoning.

## History and Evolution
Artificial intelligence evolved from symbolic reasoning and expert systems in the 1950s to statistical machine learning and modern deep artificial neural networks capable of complex perception.

## Subfields of AI
Major branches of artificial intelligence include Natural Language Processing (NLP), Computer Vision, Robotics, Automated Theorem Proving, and Reinforcement Learning.
