title: Natural Language Processing and Language Models
overview: Natural language processing (NLP) enables computers to understand, interpret, and manipulate human language text.

## Text Representation and Embeddings
NLP pipelines process raw text through tokenization, stemming, and vectorization. Traditional representations like TF-IDF and Bag-of-Words have evolved into dense vector embeddings (Word2Vec, BERT embeddings).

## Transformer Architectures
The self-attention mechanism in Transformer architectures processes tokens in parallel, capturing long-range contextual dependencies for translation, summarization, and search relevance.
