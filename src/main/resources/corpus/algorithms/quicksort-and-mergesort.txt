title: Quicksort and Merge Sort Algorithms
overview: Quicksort and Merge Sort are divide-and-conquer comparison sorting algorithms providing O(n log n) performance.

## Quicksort Pivot Selection
Quicksort partitions elements around a pivot. Randomized pivot selection or median-of-three yields expected O(n log n) time and in-place sorting.

## Merge Sort Stability
Merge Sort divides arrays into halves and merges sorted sub-arrays. It guarantees stable O(n log n) worst-case time, essential for score tie-breaking.
